digidollarstats

digidollarstats (start end timeFrame)

Returns DigiDollar activity over time as a series of fixed-length windows: how much was minted and redeemed in each window, and the supply, collateral, vault count and oracle price at the end of it. Use this for charts and trends; use getdigidollarinfo for the current state.

Requires trackdigidollar=1 in config.cfg. The series begins at the first window ending at or after DigiDollar activation (block 23,869,440), so it is empty until the activation backfill has run.

Arguments

#NameTypeRequiredDescription
1startnumericoptionalUnix time to start the series at. Defaults to the beginning of DigiDollar history.
2endnumericoptionalUnix time to end the series at. Defaults to now.
3timeFramenumericoptionalWindow length in seconds. Defaults to 86400 (one day).

Result

Returns an array of objects, one per time window, oldest first. As elsewhere, the integer fields are authoritative and the *Amount strings exist only for display.

NameTypeDescription
timenumericEnd time of the window.
supplynumericDigiDollar in circulation at window end, in cents (100 == $1.00).
supplyAmountstringThe same figure as a decimal string, for display only.
collateralnumericDGB satoshis locked in unredeemed vaults at window end.
collateralAmountstringThe same figure in DGB, for display only.
vaultsnumericUnredeemed vault count at window end.
holdersnumericAddresses holding DigiDollar at window end.
mintednumericCents minted during the window.
redeemednumericCents released by redemptions during the window.
mintsnumericNumber of mint transactions during the window.
redemptionsnumericNumber of redemptions during the window.
transfersnumericNumber of transfers during the window.
priceMinnumericLowest oracle price in the window, micro USD per DGB.
priceMaxnumericHighest oracle price in the window, micro USD per DGB.
priceAvgnumericAverage oracle price in the window, micro USD per DGB.

Examples

Daily series over all of DigiDollar history:

DigiAssetWindows-cli.exe digidollarstats

Hourly series between two times:

DigiAssetWindows-cli.exe digidollarstats 1786000000 1786900000 3600

ErrorCause
